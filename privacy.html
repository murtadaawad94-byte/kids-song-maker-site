KidsSongMaker Privacy Policy

KidsSongMaker is a privately operated content-creation tool used by its developer to upload videos to the developer's own YouTube channel.

Google and YouTube Data

KidsSongMaker uses Google OAuth 2.0 and the YouTube Data API. Authorization credentials are used only to perform actions explicitly authorized by the account owner, including uploading videos and associated metadata.

KidsSongMaker does not sell Google user data, share Google user data with advertisers, or provide Google account data to third parties.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials are stored securely for the purpose of allowing the automated uploader to access the authorized YouTube account.

Revoking Access

Access granted to KidsSongMaker can be revoked at any time through the user's Google Account security and third-party access settings.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact the developer through the contact information provided in the YouTube API audit submission.
